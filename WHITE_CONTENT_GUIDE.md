# Where to find White-exclusive content in MMBN3 Perfect

This guide only covers content that used to require White, plus the optional extras.
Everything else is where it is in vanilla Blue.

> Shop numbers (`shop 10`, etc.) are the game's internal shop index, in case you want
> to check the patch files. Area names come from the game's own area and text order.

---

## Ground Style
| What | How to get it |
|---|---|
| Ground Style (Elec/Heat/Aqua/Wood Grnd) | Through the **normal Style Change**, the same way you get every other style. White's original rule still applies: Ground points build up when you use **terrain/panel-changing chips** in battle. Shadow Style still builds up from Invis-type chips as in Blue. If Ground and Shadow are tied, the game picks either one. |

## MistMan
| What | How to get it |
|---|---|
| MistMan NPC | Appears **next to BowlMan, on his left**, in the Undernet area where you fight BowlMan for Rank 2. He shows up once the story Rank 2 battle is over, under the same conditions as BowlMan's rematches. BowlMan is still there and unchanged. |
| MistMan (V1 fight) | Talk to MistMan and answer **Yes** to "NetBattle MistMan?". You get Zenny only, the same as BowlMan's first rematch. |
| **MistMan M** chip | Beat **MistMan α**, his second rematch, which unlocks after his V1 fight. |
| **MistMan V2 / V3 / V4** chips | Beat **MistMan β**, his third rematch, available after you've beaten the game, exactly as for BowlMan. Higher busting levels give higher versions. |
| MistMan Ω | Already in vanilla Blue's random **Omega-Navi encounters** (the same lists that hold BowlMan Ω). Nothing was changed. |
| Note | MistMan and BowlMan track their progress separately. Beating one does not advance the other. |

| White Giga | Blue counterpart | How to get it |
|---|---|---|

## White-only standard chip codes from viruses
Every virus and Navi battle randomly uses **either Blue's or White's** drop table (50/50 per battle).
So any chip code that only dropped in White (for example **Cannon A/B, ShockWav H/L, Bubbler A, HeatShot B**; about 150 in total)
drops from the **same virus at the same busting level** as in White, just on roughly half of your battles.
Blue's own codes still drop from the same viruses as before.

| PanlGrab L, GrassStg \* | ACDC Square chip shop (shop 1) |
| Wind A | SciLab Square chip shop (shop 3) |

| DeltaRay Z, Balance Y | Undernet 6 BugFrag shop (shop 12) |
| Pawn N, Recov200 W, VarSwrd E, Knight V, TimeBomb M, AirSwrd R | **Higsby's Chip Order**. Their White shop was already full in Blue. As with any Chip Order, the chip has to be in your Library first. |

Prices and stock are the same as White's, with one exception: the Chip Order entries are one copy each, like every other Chip Order item.
**These shop additions only appear in a save started on this ROM.** Shop stock is set up when you press New Game.

## Already shared by both versions (nothing extra to find)
Number Trader codes, BugFrag Trader, Chip Traders, Green/Blue/Purple Mystery Data, NPC gifts, Job/BBS rewards,
Program Advances, NaviCust programs and every chip's stats are identical in Blue and White.

## Not added (cosmetic or no rewards)
- MistMan's copy in the Secret Area timed battles. The Serenade S reward is still available, as above.
- White's room decorations and their examine text, White's virus formations for five minor WWW-Navi fights, and Mr. Famous's hint-giving apprentice.

## Optional extras (not in either original version, switched on in `main.asm`)
| What | How to get it |
|---|---|
| **Punk P** chip (cut content) | Rematch Punk. **Punk** drops Zenny only. **Punk α** drops Punk P only at the **highest busting levels**. **Punk β** drops it **more often** (7 of 14 busting levels). Punk P now counts in the Mega Library, which goes up to **86**. |
| Press / EngyChng | They work in the overworld as long as you **own** them. You no longer need to install them in the NaviCust. |
